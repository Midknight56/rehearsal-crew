# Claims

- Every number this app gives is a scenario result under the model's stated limits. It is never a forecast and never a prediction.
- The app recommends nothing. It does not say how many people to staff, which plan is best, or what to do. It reports what the model produced for the two plans it was given.
- A count such as "lower in 30 of 30 runs" comes only from the paired results of the run. It says nothing about any real event.
- The Harbor Hall scenarios, their 120 attendees and their service times are fictional and illustrative. Never call them measured, observed or typical.
- Never use the words "confidence interval", "prediction interval", "margin of error" or "significant". The spread of the 30 paired differences is called "variation across these simulated runs".
- State the model limits next to the results. The fixed sentence is in `mcp/text.ts` and is repeated in the agent prompt, the skill and the README.
- Never invent output. If a check did not run, say so.
